Research

My research is mainly about evaluation and measurement for language-model systems: what a reported score actually establishes, and what it cannot.

Recently I have been studying when proxy-based validation of a score can be trusted, how much a benchmark's scoring rule rather than the agent decides the reported result, and what aggregate metrics such as a pooled AUC identify when the underlying scores are not released. Going forward, I am interested in connecting reliable diagnostics to inference-time control in reasoning and multi-agent systems.
